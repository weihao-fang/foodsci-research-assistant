Scratch closure was 56% with small molecule Q (10 µM, 18 h) and 42% with vehicle in primary human dermal fibroblasts. Donor-paired analysis (three independent donors; n = 3) gave a difference of 14 percentage points (95% CI, 2–26; P = 0.04). For each condition, the three image fields were treated as technical samples, and their values were averaged within each donor before analysis. Viability was 97% with Q and 99% with vehicle (P = 0.60). The scratch assay did not distinguish contributions from migration and proliferation.
