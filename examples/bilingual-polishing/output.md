中文

与受刺激对照相比，燕麦β-葡聚糖发酵上清冻干粉处理组RAW264.7细胞的NO归一化读数组间差值为−8.0个百分点（95% CI [−17.5, 1.5]，P=0.08），未达统计学显著性。冻干粉复溶后加入培养基，终浓度为0.40 mg/mL，暴露时间为24 h。IL-6读数未见统计学显著差异（P=0.42），细胞活力为对照的98%。统计分析以三个独立发酵批次为独立单位（n=3），每批三个技术孔先取均值。

不同批次的β-葡聚糖分析信号与较低NO读数共同变化；这一描述性关联未计算相关系数，也未进行关联检验。该模式可能为后续定向检验相关组分的作用提供线索。NF-κB通路、肽吸收及人体结局均未检测。

English

The between-group difference in the normalized NO readout in RAW264.7 cells treated with freeze-dried oat β-glucan fermentation supernatant was −8.0 percentage points relative to the stimulated control (95% CI, −17.5 to 1.5; P = 0.08) and was not statistically significant. The freeze-dried supernatant was reconstituted and added to the culture medium at a final concentration of 0.40 mg/mL for a 24 h exposure. No statistically significant difference in the IL-6 readout was detected (P = 0.42), and cell viability was 98% of the control. Statistical analysis used three independent fermentation batches as the independent units (n = 3), with the three technical wells within each batch averaged first.

Across batches, the β-glucan analytical signal co-varied with lower NO readouts; this association was descriptive, with no correlation coefficient calculated or association test performed. This pattern may warrant targeted testing of the roles of individual components. The NF-κB pathway, peptide absorption, and human outcomes were not examined.
